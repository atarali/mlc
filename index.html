About the League

What is Major League Cricket?

The fastest-growing cricket league in North America — bringing T20 cricket to millions.

🏆

Founded in 2023

MLC launched its inaugural season in 2016, making history as the first professional Twenty20 cricket league in the United States, backed by top investors and cricket boards worldwide.

Learn More
🌍

Global Star Power

MLC features world-class cricketers from Australia, Bangladesh, England, West Indies, and beyond. Top ICC-ranked players compete alongside American talent.
